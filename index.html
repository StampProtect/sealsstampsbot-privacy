Privacy Policy — SealsStampsBotPin

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Pinterest app is used only by the owner of the Pinterest account to publish and manage Pins and boards for the Seals & Stamps vector stamp and seal layouts catalog.

What we access

With your permission through Pinterest OAuth we request these scopes: user_accounts:read, boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write. They allow us to list and create boards and Pins, and to read Pin and account analytics.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect payment details, email addresses, or personal data of other Pinterest users.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer data to third parties.
  • We do not use this access for advertising campaigns.

Storage and revocation

The access token is stored locally on the owner's computer and is not sent to third parties. You can revoke access at any time in Pinterest settings under “Apps and websites with access”.
